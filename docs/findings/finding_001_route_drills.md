Finding 001
Title
Wide Receiver Route Drills May Be More Informative Than Traditional Combine Drills

Date
2026-10-14

Evidence
The most frequent WR drills are:

GAUNTLET_DRILL
POST_CORNER_ROUTE_RIGHT
DAGGER_ROUTE_LEFT
GO_ROUTE_RIGHT
SLOT_SAIL_ROUTE_RIGHT
These drills resemble real NFL route-running situations more closely than traditional agility drills.

Implication
Route-specific drills may contain stronger predictive signals for future NFL separation than generic drills such as the 40-Yard Dash or Three Cone Drill.

Status
Preliminary Finding

Finding 014
Title
Individual Route Drills May Measure Different Athletic Skills

Context
Validation across multiple drills produced inconsistent relationships between CODE Score V3 and NFL career outcomes.

POST_CORNER_ROUTE_RIGHT showed a positive association with career usage.

COMEBACK_ROUTE_RIGHT showed a negative association.

These findings suggest the possibility that different route drills capture different athletic dimensions.

Observation
The assumption of a universal route-execution metric does not appear to hold across drills.

Instead, route-specific skill requirements may drive the observed relationships.

Ability to execute complex routes using controlled movement paths.

Working Hypothesis
Different combine drills capture different movement skills.

NFL success may be associated with specific skills rather than a single universal route-execution score.

Implications
Future validation should focus on:

Individual skills.
Route-specific metrics.
Cross-drill comparisons.
NFL career outcomes.
